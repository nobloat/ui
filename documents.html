How invoices are sent

An invoice leaves the system as a PDF attached to an email. It is sent once you finalize it, never before, and a copy goes to the archive with the same number.

Before you finalize

  1. Check the address and VAT id of the customer.
  2. Check every line and the due date.
  3. Choose who receives the email; the billing contact of the customer is preset.

What happens then

The invoice gets the next free number of its series, which can never change again. The mail waits in the outbox until the mail server accepts it, and is retried if it does not.

Sending again

Open the invoice and choose Send again. The same PDF goes out once more; the number and the date stay the same.
