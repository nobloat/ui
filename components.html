Rules

Every button carries an icon before its text, drawn at the size of the text so the two line up; a button with only an icon is round and is named for screen readers and in its tooltip. A page has at most one primary button, the one action most people come for, and a dangerous action always asks first, naming what will be lost. Buttons are one height, 40 px, and only dense rows and tables use the small one.

On a phone, a shortcut is no longer shown, create, edit, delete and copy shrink to their icon while keeping their name for screen readers, and the primary button spans the width. Hover, focus, pressed, disabled and busy are not variants anyone picks: every button has them, and they follow from its colours.
