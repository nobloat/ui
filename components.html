Rules

every button has an icon before its text, at text size; icon-only buttons are round and named (aria-label, title)
at most one primary button per page; on phones it spans the width
create, edit, delete and copy show only their icon on phones (class phone-icon); the text stays for screen readers
a shortcut shows as a key hint after the text on desktop, never on phones
danger always asks for confirmation, naming the object and the consequence
one height: size-control (40 px); small (28 px) only in dense rows
states are shown, never picked: hover, focus, pressed, disabled, busy
