Icon styles

directions to choose from; not part of the reference yet

The current set is a clean outline style, but it reads like Lucide or Feather. Four directions, from parameters only (A–C, the whole set) to a redrawn signature (D, a prototype of 16 icons).

A · current: 1.5 stroke, round ends, round joins, circles

{{range icons}}{{iconsvg . "1.5" "round" "round"}}{{end}}

B · precise: 1.25 stroke, square ends, sharp joins (technical, like a plan)

{{range icons}}{{iconsvg . "1.25" "square" "miter"}}{{end}}

C · bold: 2 stroke, round ends (friendly, reads well at 12 px)

{{range icons}}{{iconsvg . "2" "round" "round"}}{{end}}

D · angular (prototype): no curves, only 90° and 45°, square ends, octagons instead of circles

Matches the monospace type and the ][ mark; nobody else ships it, so it becomes the nobloat look. Needs every icon redrawn on the grid.

Further ideas to combine

  • Bracket frames: containers (calendar, file, folder, mail, copy) drawn as [ ] with open corners, like the logo.
  • One accent dot: every icon carries one small filled dot that marks its point (the search lens, the tag hole, the key hole); with the sprite the dot can take --accent, like the logo character.
  • Fixed 2 px grid: all points on even coordinates, so 16 px and 32 px stay crisp on 1× screens.
