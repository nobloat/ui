Icons

{{len icons}} icons, 16×16, 1.25 stroke, square ends, sharp joins, in currentColor

Use like a font: <i class="icon-cog" aria-hidden="true"></i>. The size is the font size, the color is the text color. An icon that carries meaning alone gets role="img" aria-label="…"; an icon-only button gets aria-label on the button. Also as a sprite (<svg width="1em" height="1em"><use href="/static/icons.svg#cog"/></svg>, for places without ui.css) and as single files in icons/.

In buttons and elsewhere

An icon goes before the text, the same size as the text; an icon alone needs aria-label on its button or link. Create, edit, delete and copy get their icon from the class alone (.new .edit .del .copy).
